# growth-test-planner

Public repo, MIT. Live: https://planner.theoutlines.xyz. Author: Ivan Polushin (theoutlines).

**Project notes are private.** Before working here, read `planner-notes/CLAUDE.md` in the private repo
`theoutlines/theoutlines-site` (attach it to the session). Write every new decision, status or stat
there, never in this repo: anything committed here is public, history included.

Never commit to this repo: plans, prices, traffic or Search Console numbers, account or email details,
names of people asked for quotes, reports, prompts. CI (`tools/public_guard.py`) fails on them and on a
CLAUDE.md longer than 3 KB.

Public design rules for new pages: `DESIGN_RULES.md`.
