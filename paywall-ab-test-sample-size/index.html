How many users do you need for a paywall A/B test?

It depends on the change you want to catch more than on anything else. Halve the change and you need four times the people. For a typical paywall, catching +10% in paid conversion takes about 56,000 people in each version; catching +5% takes about 220,000.

When the number is out of reach

  • Test a bolder change. A +20% change needs a sixteenth of the people a +5% change does. Copy tweaks rarely move revenue that much; plans, prices and the trial offer can.
  • Decide on trial starts first. They need about 40% of the people that paid conversion does; check paid once the last trials bill.
  • Keep it to two versions and split 50/50. A third version or an 80/20 split costs people you may not have.
  • Run longer, in whole weeks. Divide the people you need by the people who see the paywall each week.
