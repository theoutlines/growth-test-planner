A/B testing a paywall with a free trial: when can you decide?

Not when the traffic stops. Wait for the longest free trial among the plans people buy to end, then about a week for refunds. Until then, a trial start isn't a payment and the test can't tell you which version earns more.

When the decision day falls

A 4-week paywall test that starts on Monday, November 9:

Free trialTraffic endsDecide on
NoneDec 6Mon, Dec 14
3 daysDec 6Thu, Dec 17
7 daysDec 6Mon, Dec 21
14 daysDec 6Mon, Dec 28

The week after the last trials bill is for refunds: a version that wins on first payments can still lose once refunds land.

Why waiting matters

  • Trials aren't payments. A version can win trial starts by making the price harder to see and then lose on paid. Read the result on paid conversion or revenue per viewer.
  • The last trials bill late. Someone who starts a 7-day trial on the last day of the test pays a week later.
  • Refunds come after. Watch refunds as a guardrail: if B's refund share is clearly higher, its win may not hold.
  • Testing the trial itself? Adding a trial, changing its length or putting it on the annual plan changes who pays and when; judge it on revenue per paywall viewer after the longest trial has billed.
