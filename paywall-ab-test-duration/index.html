How long should a paywall A/B test run?

Whole weeks of traffic until your test can see the change you expect, then the longest free trial, then a week for refunds. For most subscription apps that is 4 to 8 weeks of traffic.

  1. 1
    Traffic: 2 to 8 whole weeksUntil the smallest change your test can catch is smaller than the change you expect.
  2. 2
    Plus the longest free trialPeople who start a trial on the last day pay only when it ends.
  3. 3
    Plus about 7 days for refundsA win on first payments can still lose once refunds land.

4 weeks of traffic with a 7-day trial: start Mon, Nov 16, decide Mon, Dec 28.

Plan your paywall test

How to pick the length

  1. Start from the change you expect. If your test only catches +9.1% or more, a headline tweak won’t show. Test a bolder change.
  2. Run whole weeks. Weekdays and weekends convert differently; a test that stops mid-week reads that as an effect.
  3. Add the trial and the refunds. With a 3-day trial, decide 3 + 7 days after the traffic stops.
  4. Don’t stop early on a good day. Pick the length first, then wait.

Judge on revenue or on conversion?

Prices, plans or trial changes: revenue per viewer, because conversion can fall while revenue rises. Copy and layout changes: paid conversion answers faster, and catches +7.5% in 4 weeks where revenue catches +9.1%.
