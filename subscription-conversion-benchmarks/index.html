Why RevenueCat and Adapty conversion numbers don’t match

“Conversion” means a different thing in each report. RevenueCat counts per install or per trial start. Adapty’s paywall report counts per paywall view. Adapty’s subscriptions report counts per install. Compare your number only with one that has the same thing below the line.

Where people mix them up

  • Per view is not per install. A paywall where 2.75% of viewers pay is a typical US paywall. An app where 2.75% of installs pay is above RevenueCat’s 2.0% median. Same number, different stories, because many installs never see a paywall and some see it several times.
  • Trial → paid differs between reports. 32.2% at RevenueCat, 25.59% at Adapty. Different customers, different samples. Neither is wrong; pick one and stay with it.
  • Hard paywalls sit in another league. In RevenueCat’s data, apps with a hard paywall convert 10.7% of installs to paid by day 35, against 2.1% for freemium. Compare a hard-paywall app with the 10.7%, not the overall median.
  • Read the chart, not only the summary. RevenueCat’s AI chapter calls 8.5% vs 5.6% “trial-to-paid”. The charts show it is the trial start rate.

How to compare your number

  • Write down what is below the line: installs, trial starts, paywall views, or people who saw the paywall.
  • Pick the report that counts the same way, then the closest category and region.
  • Place your number against the median and the top quartile, not against one figure from a blog post.

The planner works per person who saw the paywall, so its defaults come from Adapty’s per-view medians.
